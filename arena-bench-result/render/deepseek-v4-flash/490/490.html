Pro Tips for Pet Parents

🐕 Regular Vet Checkups

Annual exams help catch health issues early. Keep a record of vaccinations and treatments.

🥗 Balanced Nutrition

Choose high‑quality food suited to your pet’s age, size, and activity level. Always provide fresh water.

🏃 Daily Exercise

Regular walks and playtime keep your pet fit and mentally stimulated. Aim for at least 30 min a day.
