Welcome to Sawasdee

Step into Sawasdee Thai Cuisine and embark on a culinary journey to the heart of Thailand. Our chefs meticulously craft each dish using traditional recipes and the freshest ingredients, bringing you an authentic taste of Thai culture. From the vibrant spices of a Green Curry to the comforting sweetness of Pad Thai, every bite is a celebration of flavor.

We believe in creating more than just a meal; we offer an experience – a warm, inviting atmosphere perfect for family dinners, romantic evenings, or casual gatherings. Come and discover your new favorite Thai dish!

Our Delicious Menu

Pad Thai

Our signature stir-fried rice noodles with shrimp or chicken, tofu, peanuts, bean sprouts, and lime.

Green Curry

Aromatic green curry paste cooked with coconut milk, bamboo shoots, eggplant, bell peppers, and your choice of protein.

Tom Yum Goong

Spicy and sour shrimp soup infused with lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime leaves, and mushrooms.
